Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

In short

Every plate is custom-made to your exact text and style, so we cannot accept returns or exchanges simply because you changed your mind. We will always make it right if your plate arrives damaged, faulty, or different from what you ordered.

1. Cancellations

Because production starts shortly after payment is confirmed, you may cancel and receive a full refund only if you contact us within 2 hours of placing your order, before it has entered production. Once a plate has entered production, it cannot be cancelled, as it has already been made specifically for you and cannot be resold.

2. Change of mind

As each plate is personalised with the text and style you chose, we are unable to offer a refund or exchange for a correctly-made order simply because you no longer want it, ordered the wrong text or style by mistake, or found a cheaper alternative elsewhere. Please check your text, spelling, and style selection carefully before completing payment.

3. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items

If your plate arrives damaged in transit, has a manufacturing fault, or does not match the text/style you actually ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item. We will offer, at our discretion, a free replacement or a full refund.

4. Lost or undelivered orders

If tracking shows your order as delivered but you did not receive it, or if it is lost in transit beyond our stated delivery window, contact us and we will investigate with our courier partner and offer a replacement or refund once loss is confirmed.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout via Razorpay, and typically appear within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank or card issuer.

6. How to reach us

For any cancellation, damage, or order issue, contact us via the details on our homepage with your order number so we can help quickly.
